# Favorites

!!! info "Concept — Pin frequently used pages and reach them with one click"

With **Favorites** you create your personal shortcuts:
pages you need every day — such as a particular calculation or the
customer list — then appear at the very top of the navigation and as cards on
the [home page](home.md).

## Pinning a favorite { #einen-favoriten-anpinnen }

=== "Via the star on the calculation tile"

    Every calculation tile — in the [calculation overview](../calculations/index.md)
    as well as on the group pages *Material*, *Product* and *Production* — has
    a **star**. Clicking the empty star makes the calculation a
    favorite, clicking the filled star removes it again — without
    going through the navigation and without opening the calculation.

=== "Via the navigation"

    1. In the [navigation](navigation.md), click the desired entry with the
       **right mouse button** (e.g. *Calculations > Product >
       Braiding angle*).
    2. In the context menu, select **Add to favorites**.

    This method works for all pages, not only for calculations.

The entry immediately appears in the **Favorites** category at the top
of the navigation tree — with the same icon and name as the original. Clicking
it opens the same page.

!!! info "Single pages only"
    You can pin single pages (end points of the tree). Groups such as
    *Master data* or *Calculations* cannot be added to favorites as a
    whole.

## Removing a favorite { #einen-favoriten-entfernen }

Click the filled **star** on the calculation tile — or click the entry with the
**right mouse button** (in the favorites area or at its
original location) and select **Remove favorite**.

## Where favorites appear { #wo-favoriten-erscheinen }

* **Navigation:** the *Favorites* category at the very top — this is where your
  pinned entries are collected.
* **Home page:** the *Favorites* card with one Herzog-blue tile per entry
  (icon, name and source area), see [Home page (Home)](home.md).

## Good to know { #gut-zu-wissen }

* **Personal and per workspace:** Favorites belong to the signed-in
  user and apply per workspace — each colleague maintains their own
  list, and it is kept across program restarts.
* **Permissions take precedence:** If your [role](../admin/roles.md) loses
  the permission for a favorite page, the favorite is hidden
  automatically. It reappears as soon as the permission is restored.
* **Desktop app only:** The [web app](../web/index.md) has no
  favorites; there you find every calculator via the search in the
  calculator overview.
