# Product

!!! abstract "Reference — Overview of the calculations relating to the finished product"

Calculations relating to the finished product – braiding angle, braid density,
diameter, length, weight and the two-layer core-cover product. For
tubular and hose-shaped braids there is a separate subgroup,
[Hollow braid](../tubular-braid/index.md).
