# Contact support

!!! question "Troubleshooting — When these help pages don't help"

If the other pages in [Help](index.md) have not solved your problem,
you can reach Herzog support in the following ways.

| Route | Details |
|---|---|
| **Email** | [support@herzog-online.com](mailto:support@herzog-online.com) |
| **Phone** | +49 441 3008-0 |
| **Fax** | +49 441 3008-100 |
| **Postal address** | Herzog GmbH, Am Alexanderhaus 160, D-26127 Oldenburg |

## In the program { #im-programm }

* The menu *Help > Feedback and News* opens the public
  feedback page in the browser – there you can report errors or
  submit suggestions for improvement.
* The menu *Help > About Herzog CAB* shows your installed **version number**
  and the contact details at a glance.

## What you should have ready for a quick answer { #was-sie-fur-eine-schnelle-antwort-bereithalten-sollten }

Please state with every request:

* Your **version number** (see *Help > About Herzog CAB*).
* Your **operating system** (Windows 10 / 11).
* A short description of what you did and what happened
  instead – ideally with a screenshot.
* For licence problems: the **container serial number** from the CodeMeter
  Control Center (see [Licence problems](license-problems.md)).
