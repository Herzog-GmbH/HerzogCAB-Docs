*[carrier]: Bobbin holder on the braiding machine; carries a bobbin with braiding material along the track.
*[carriers]: Bobbin holders on the braiding machine; each carries a bobbin with braiding material along the track.
*[horngear]: Rotating gear of the braiding machine; its slots pick up the carriers and pass them on to the next horngear.
*[horngears]: Rotating gears of the braiding machine; their slots pick up the carriers and pass them on to the next horngear.
*[track]: The path along which the carriers run through the braiding machine.
*[titer]: Fineness of a yarn, given in tex or dtex.
*[tex]: Unit of fineness: grams per 1000 meters of yarn.
*[dtex]: Unit of fineness: grams per 10000 meters of yarn.
*[ply count]: Number of individual threads running in parallel per carrier.
*[braid density]: Number of braid crossings per length of the product.
*[braiding angle]: Angle between the braiding thread and the product axis.
*[hollow braid]: Braid without a core - hollow inside.
*[Workspace]: Central data folder of Herzog CAB (master data, orders, designs, users).
*[workspace folder]: Central data folder of Herzog CAB (master data, orders, designs, users).
*[CodeMeter]: Licensing software by Wibu-Systems; manages the Herzog CAB licence (dongle or software container).
*[winding head]: Single winding position on a bobbin winding machine.
*[machine sheet]: Printout of an order with all setup data for the machine.
